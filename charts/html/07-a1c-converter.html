A1C & Average Glucose

A1C estimates your average blood glucose over ~2–3 months. Estimated average glucose (eAG) translates A1C into the same units as your meter.

Many adults’ A1C goal

< 7%

Roughly eAG under ~154 mg/dL. Goals may be tighter or looser depending on hypoglycemia risk, age, and other factors.

Why meter & A1C can disagree
  • A1C is a long-term average
  • Anemia, kidney disease, pregnancy can skew A1C
  • Recent highs/lows may not yet show
  • CGM time-in-range complements A1C
