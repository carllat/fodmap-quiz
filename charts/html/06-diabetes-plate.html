Diabetes Plate Method

A simple visual for building balanced meals without weighing every gram. Use a 9-inch plate. Adjust portions with your dietitian if you use insulin dosing or specific carb goals.

½ Non-starchy veg
  • Broccoli, greens, salad
  • Peppers, cucumber, zucchini
  • Cauliflower, green beans
  • Tomatoes, mushrooms
¼ Lean protein
  • Chicken, turkey, fish
  • Eggs, tofu, tempeh
  • Lean beef or pork
  • Cheese, cottage cheese
¼ Carbohydrate
  • Whole grains, pasta, rice
  • Starchy veg (potato, corn)
  • Beans, lentils
  • Fruit or yogurt
Build-a-meal examples
MealPlate idea
Lunch Salad greens + grilled chicken + quinoa
Dinner Roasted veg + salmon + sweet potato
Breakfast* Veg omelet + berries + toast

*Breakfast often needs a flexible plate — emphasize protein + fiber.

Extra steady tips
  • Fill the veg half first
  • Choose water over sugary drinks
  • Use smaller plates if portions creep up
  • Beans can count as protein or carb — pick one “slot”
  • Still count carbs if you dose insulin

Educational reference only — not medical advice. Adapted from widely used diabetes plate education models. Individual nutrition plans differ.
