Glycemic Index Food Guide

Glycemic index (GI) ranks carb foods by how quickly they raise blood glucose. Lower GI choices often produce a gentler rise — but portion size (glycemic load) still matters.

Low GI ≤55
  • Steel-cut / rolled oats
  • Barley, bulgur, quinoa
  • Most beans & lentils
  • Non-starchy vegetables
  • Apple, berries, orange
  • Greek yogurt, milk
  • Nuts, peanuts
  • 100% stone-ground bread*
Medium GI 56–69
  • Brown rice
  • Couscous
  • Whole-wheat bread*
  • Quick oats
  • Sweet potato
  • Banana (ripe)
  • Pineapple
  • Popcorn
High GI ≥70
  • White bread, bagels
  • White rice, rice cakes
  • Instant potatoes
  • Cornflakes / puffed cereals
  • Pretzels, crackers
  • Watermelon
  • Sugary drinks
  • Many baked desserts
Practical ways to lower the rise
  • Pair carbs with protein, fat, or fiber
  • Choose intact grains over refined
  • Prefer whole fruit over juice
  • Cook pasta al dente; cool & reheat rice/pasta when helpful
  • Watch portions even on low-GI foods
  • Vinegar or lemon with meals may blunt spikes for some
  • Walk 10–15 minutes after eating
  • Compare your CGM/meter — personal responses vary
GI is a tool, not a rulebook Ripeness, cooking method, processing, and what else is on the plate change GI. Total carbs and your medication plan remain the foundation. *Bread GI varies widely by brand and grain structure.

Educational reference only — not medical advice. GI values are approximate published averages and can vary by source and preparation.
