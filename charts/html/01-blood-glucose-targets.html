Low — treat now
Below 70 mg/dL
Hypoglycemia
  • Treat with 15g fast-acting carbs
  • Recheck in 15 minutes
  • Repeat if still under 70
High — take action
Above 250 mg/dL
Check ketones
  • Drink water; follow care plan
  • Test for ketones if instructed
  • Call clinician if rising or ill
