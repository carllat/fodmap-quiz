Carb Counting Pocket Guide

Carbohydrates raise blood glucose the most. Counting grams helps match food to insulin or medication plans. Values are typical averages — always check labels.

Label reading tips
  • Use Total Carbohydrate, not just sugar
  • Multiply by the servings you eat
  • Fiber & sugar alcohols: ask your educator how you count them
  • “Net carbs” marketing varies — verify math

*Meal/snack targets vary widely. Work with a dietitian for your insulin-to-carb ratio or medication plan.
